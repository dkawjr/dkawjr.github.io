About Adrian

I’m D.K. Adrian Williams Jr., an MD–PhD candidate in the Medical Scientist Training Program at Albert Einstein College of Medicine. I study computational neuroscience in the Coen-Cagli Laboratory, where I use EEG, behavior, eye tracking, and computational models to investigate visual perception in autism.

My collaborative research examines physiological signals collected before, during, and after surgery. I work on capnography, pulse oximetry, EEG, and wearable near-infrared measurements, with an emphasis on reproducible analyses and careful evaluation of clinical AI.

I founded Afferent Health to provide scientific consulting, data analytics, and AI evaluation. I also teach and develop courses in information technology, healthcare management, and global leadership.

Education

  • MD–PhD training, Albert Einstein College of Medicine, 2022–present. Doctoral research in computational neuroscience and systems biology.
  • NIH Oxford-Cambridge (OxCam) Scholars Program, accepted to the MD–PhD program in 2022 before matriculating at Einstein.
  • MSc in Biomedical Sciences, Albert Einstein College of Medicine, May 2025. Focus in neuroscience; doctoral qualifying examination completed.
  • BS in Neuroscience, Wright State University, 2021. Magna cum laude; spring commencement speaker.

Earlier research

My training includes computational neuroimaging in Conor Liston’s laboratory at Weill Cornell Medicine, computational neurobiology in Jeremy Gunawardena’s laboratory at Harvard Medical School, and medical-imaging research in Timothy Duong’s laboratory at Einstein.
