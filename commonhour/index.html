What we do

We help programs make the schedule they meant to make.

Compliance is the floor. Context, fairness, and human judgment stay visible.

01

Remember every rule.

Commonhour keeps ACGME, specialty, institutional, program, rotation, supervision, and local fairness rules in one readable Rulebook.

02

See the whole sequence.

It catches patterns a simple hours check can miss: the intern alone on nights, the 24 followed by ICU, the didactics-to-night squeeze, the fourth bad weekend.

03

Keep people in control.

The chief edits. The program director reviews the change. The schedule publishes only after a named human approves it.

How it works

Four steps. No rip-and-replace.

Start with one block. Import what already exists. Prove that it works before asking the program to change anything else.

01

Teach it your program.

Import the roster and current schedule. Start from a specialty template, then approve the local rules that actually govern your program.

Rulebook sessionChief + coordinator + PD · about 60 minutes
02

Residents request what matters.

Each resident gets a secure link. They choose must-have, important, or nice-to-have—without another account or an email scavenger hunt.

Simple by designRequest link · receipt · clear deadline
03

Compare clear drafts.

Commonhour returns a few feasible schedules with different tradeoffs: most stable, best request fit, or best burden balance.

No mystery scoreRules, alternatives, and burden counts stay visible
04

Review, approve, then sync.

The chief edits in a familiar grid. The PD reviews only the changes and exceptions. Approved schedules update Apple, Google, or Outlook calendars.

Important boundaryDrafts never reach resident calendars

Acknowledge

Not a black box

The system shows its work.

The schedule is generated by explicit constraints and visible tradeoffs—not a generative model making unreviewable decisions.

Rulebook
Every rule has an owner and source.

ACGME, specialty, institution, program, rotation, or a documented exception.

Receipt
Every assignment has a reason.

See eligibility, supervision, adjacent shifts, preferences, alternatives, and fairness impact.

Approval
Every published version has a human name.

Overrides require a reason. Calendars update only after approval. Every version can be rolled back.

commonhour

A working product concept for transparent, specialty-aware residency scheduling. The name is provisional pending trademark and domain clearance.

Commonhour would check a draft against the ACGME, specialty, institutional, and program rules configured in the approved Rulebook. It cannot guarantee accreditation compliance or observe unreported work. Final compliance and approval remain the program’s responsibility. Current ACGME requirements.
