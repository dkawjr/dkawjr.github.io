§ 1   About

(not loaded)

Adrian is an MD-PhD student in the NIH-funded Medical Scientist Training Program at Albert Einstein College of Medicine. His PhD work in the Coen-Cagli Lab sits where computational neuroscience, EEG-based neuroimaging, and machine learning meet — decoding real-time visual segmentation in autism to surface neural biomarkers for early diagnosis and precision subtyping.

Beyond the lab he is a builder and consultant. As COO and co-founder of Focused Future LLC, he develops AI-powered tools for mental health assessment. He has advised executives, written and taught coursework across universities, and held leadership posts spanning faculty senate, review boards, and national advocacy — from the Ohio statehouse to the World Health Assembly in Geneva. He is a 2025 Cell Rising Black Scientist Awardee (Honorable Mention), a Harvard Medical School research fellow, and a Weill Cornell Leadership Alliance alumnus.

§ 2   Research interests

  1. Computational Neuroelectrophysiology. Decoding real-time visual processing in autism spectrum disorder using EEG and machine learning to uncover neural biomarkers for diagnosis and precision subtyping.
  2. AI & Machine Learning in Healthcare. Deep learning architectures for medical imaging, clinical decision support, and large language model evaluation across specialties.
  3. Biomedical Imaging. Computational approaches to neuroimaging (EEG/fMRI/DTI) and digital pathology for cancer detection and neurodevelopmental research.
  4. Clinical Informatics & Health Innovation. EHR-based research, automated research platforms, and digital health tools bridging data science with patient outcomes and equity.
  5. Translational Psychiatry. Investigating ketamine's network-plasticity effects and psychiatric comorbidities in critical care settings.
  6. Health Disparities & Public Health. Sociodemographic factors in medical education, retention initiatives, and equitable access to emerging healthcare technology.
