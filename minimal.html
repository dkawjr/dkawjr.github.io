Physician-scientist in the making

Adrian is an MD-PhD student in the NIH-funded Medical Scientist Training Program at Albert Einstein College of Medicine. His PhD work in the Coen-Cagli Lab sits where computational neuroscience, EEG-based neuroimaging, and machine learning meet — decoding real-time visual segmentation in autism to surface neural biomarkers for early diagnosis and precision subtyping.

Beyond the lab he is a builder and consultant. As COO and co-founder of Focused Future LLC, he develops AI-powered tools for mental health assessment. He has advised executives, written and taught coursework across universities, and held leadership posts spanning faculty senate, review boards, and national advocacy — from the Ohio statehouse to the World Health Assembly in Geneva. He is a 2025 Cell Rising Black Scientist Awardee (Honorable Mention), a Harvard Medical School research fellow, and a Weill Cornell Leadership Alliance alumnus.
