Research Software & Projects

Afferent Research

A research workspace for anesthesiology and critical-care projects, with dataset inventory, study-planning support, cohort exploration, exploratory analysis, and reproducibility bundles. The workspace connects research questions to recorded methods and saved outputs.

Inquire about Afferent Research

Aging Suite

A research prototype for exploring laboratory and ECG-based age estimates in a preoperative workflow. The demonstration uses fictional patients and includes source information, local reports, and browser-based ECG inference. It is a research demonstration; prospective clinical validation and live EHR integration remain future work.

Request more information about Aging Suite

EEG analysis and teaching

Reproducible workflows for EEG preprocessing, artifact review, multivariate decoding, statistical testing, and visualization, developed alongside my doctoral research. Teaching materials introduce trainees to these methods through structured exercises.

Physiological signal analysis

Ongoing methods work spans capnogram morphology, optical waveforms, anesthetic EEG, and wearable near-infrared recordings. I develop shared quality-control and analysis tools to support these studies.
